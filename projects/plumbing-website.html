Plumbing Business Website

A responsive website for Strictly Plumbing Solutions, a Cape Town plumbing business, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Live demo (opens in a new tab) View code on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

The problem

A local trade business has to earn trust quickly online. Customers want to know which services are offered, see proof of past work and get in touch for a quote, usually from their phone.

The goal was a website for Strictly Plumbing Solutions that answers those questions on one page and makes the next step, requesting a quote, obvious from the first screen.

My role

I designed and built the website, from page structure and visual style to the interactive features, and published it on Netlify.

What the site includes

The site is a single page organised around the questions a customer asks, in the order they ask them.

  • Hero with the business promise and two calls to action: request a quote or explore services.
  • About section covering the company's mission and core expertise.
  • Services covering leak repairs, pipe installation, drain cleaning, water heater service, Certificates of Compliance (CoC) and construction management.
  • Project gallery of completed work, including bathroom renovation, commercial kitchen installation, leak repair and water heater installation, with a scrollable slider and an inline video.
  • Client testimonials in a rotating carousel.
  • Contact details for calling, emailing and visiting, plus a quote request form where customers choose the service they need.

Technical decisions

HTML, CSS and JavaScript
A small business site doesn't need a framework. Plain files keep the site fast, easy to maintain and free to host as a static site on Netlify.
One-page layout
All content sits on one page with smooth-scrolling navigation, so customers can move between services, work and contact details without page loads.
Gallery slider
Arrow buttons scroll the gallery one project card at a time, keeping several projects available without making the page very long.
Inline video
Project videos play and pause when clicked, and starting one video pauses any other, so only one plays at a time.
Rotating testimonials
Testimonials change every five seconds, showing several reviews in the space of one.

Next steps

The quote request form currently confirms submissions in the browser. The next step is connecting it to an email or form-handling service so that requests reach the business directly.
